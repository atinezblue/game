Privacy Policy

Make 10: Number Blocks

Effective date: 18 August 2026

Support

I am Dat Vu, an independent developer, and I make Make 10: Number Blocks on my own. This policy explains what the game does with information, in plain terms. It is short because the game collects nothing.

The short version

The game has no server of mine, no account, no login, and no advertising. Everything it knows about you — your progress, your scores, your settings — lives on your phone and I never see it. The one thing that can ever leave your device is a single number, your best Endless score, and only to Apple Game Center, and only after you switch Game Center on yourself. The App Store privacy label for this app is Data Not Collected, and that is the literal truth: the app contains no networking code of its own at all.

What stays on your device

One small save file on your phone holds your best scores, your recent runs, your current run, your settings (sound, vibration, reduce motion, colourblind marks, appearance, language), and how far you got through the first-run tutorial. I do not receive any of it unless you paste it into an email to me.

You can wipe all of it at any time with Settings → Erase my data.

Game Center, if you turn it on

What it sends, and to whom

Game Center is off when you install the game. While it is off the game never even asks iOS to sign you in, so no sign-in banner appears and nothing at all is sent.

If you switch it on in Settings → Game Center, the game asks iOS to sign you in with your Apple Account and posts one integer — your best Endless score — to the Apple leaderboard make10.endless. That is the entire payload. There is no nickname, no message, no profile, and no second leaderboard.

The name and photo shown next to that score on the board are your Game Center alias, which belongs to your Apple Account. I do not choose it, cannot change it, and never receive it. The game has no way to read the leaderboard back — the world board you see is Apple's own screen, opened inside the game.

Because there is no text you can type and no profile you can fill in, there is no user-generated content in this game to moderate or report.

How to delete it

A Game Center score belongs to your Apple Account, not to me — I have no server holding a copy and no way to reach into Apple's board. To remove it, use iOS Settings ▸ Game Center on your device, which is where Apple lets you manage or delete your Game Center data. Turning the switch off inside the game stops any further posting.

To delete everything on the device itself, use Settings → Erase my data in the game, or delete the app.

Apple's privacy information: apple.com/legal/privacy

No ads, no purchases, no analytics

The game shows no advertising. There is no ad network in it, so no advertising identifier, no ad profile, and no ad measurement. The game never asks for iOS tracking permission, because it has nothing to track you with.

There are no in-app purchases and no subscriptions. Nothing in the game costs money after you have it.

There is no analytics SDK. The game notes what happened during a session in memory so the code can reason about the run; that note is never written to disk and never sent anywhere, and it disappears when you close the app.

If you email me

If you write to me for support, I process what you send — your email address, device and app details, screenshots, and your message — to answer you and to look into problems. I keep support emails for 12 months unless I have a legal or security reason to keep them longer.

What the game never asks for

There is no account of mine and no login. The game does not ask for your real name, email address, contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone, health data, bank or card details, or passwords. It does not read your device identifiers and it does not use the network for anything of its own.

Why I process this, and on what basis

The only processing that happens off your device is the Game Center score, and it happens because you switched it on: I rely on your consent for that, and you can withdraw it at any time with the same switch. Support email is processed to answer the request you made of me. Nothing else is processed at all.

Who else is involved

Only Apple — App Store distribution, and Game Center for the optional leaderboard. There is no third-party SDK, no advertising partner, no analytics vendor, and no database of mine.

I do not sell personal information, and I do not share it for advertising.

Where the data goes

Apple may process Game Center information in countries other than the one you live in, under Apple's own terms and transfer protections. I hold no data of yours anywhere, so there is nothing of mine to transfer.

Security

The save file sits in the app's own sandboxed storage, which iOS protects along with the rest of your device. The Game Center connection is Apple's, over their own secured transport. Since I run no server and store nothing, there is no database of mine to be breached. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and I will not pretend otherwise.

Children

The game is not aimed at children below the minimum digital-consent age in their country, and it does not collect personal information from anyone, children included. If you have a concern, email me at datvu.meo@gmail.com.

Your choices

  • Play with Game Center off — that is how it arrives, and it stays off until you turn it on.
  • Turn it off at any time in Settings → Game Center; nothing further is posted.
  • Erase every score and setting on the device with Settings → Erase my data.
  • Manage or delete your Game Center data in iOS Settings ▸ Game Center.
  • Email me with a question, a request, or a complaint about any of this.
  • Complain to your local data-protection authority if you are in a country that has one.

Deleting the app removes the local save the way iOS normally does. It does not remove a Game Center score, because that score is part of your Apple Account rather than part of the app.

Changes

I will update this page when the game, its providers, or the law changes, and the new effective date will appear at the top.
