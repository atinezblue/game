Frequently asked

How do I play?

Press anywhere on the screen and hold — an invisible line lifts the bottle. Drag to swing it, then release to throw. You win a Travel level when the bottle flips at least once in the air and comes to rest upright inside the landing area.

Which levels are free?

Levels 1–10 are free. From level 11 onwards, only odd-numbered levels ask for one optional rewarded ad, and that ad unlocks the level permanently — replaying it never asks again. Even-numbered levels never ask for an ad at all. Rush mode is always free and never asks for an ad.

I bought Full World Unlock but it is gone

Open Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. The Game also re-checks your App Store entitlements every launch, so a reinstall normally restores the purchase on its own.

The ad does not load

Ads come from Google and are not always available. Check your connection and try again later. You are never stuck: the same unlock card also lets you buy Full World Unlock, and Rush mode plus the first ten levels never need an ad.

How do I join or leave the Global leaderboard?

It is off until you accept the consent card the first time you open Rankings. You can turn it on or off at any time in Settings → Global leaderboard. Turning it off deletes your published record from my server.

Someone is using an offensive nickname

Tap that player’s row on the leaderboard, then choose Report this name. That hides them from your view straight away and sends the report to me for review. Hide this player hides them without filing a report.

How do I delete my data?

Turn Settings → Global leaderboard off — that erases your published record. For anything else, email me with your Player ID and I will handle it within 30 days. See the Privacy Policy.

My nickname was refused

Nicknames must be 2–20 characters, must not contain offensive language, and must not look like an email address, phone number, or link. The leaderboard is public, so please do not use your real name.
